Job Experience

My work experience has provided me with valuable insight into responsibility, teamwork, and leadership. I currently work in a supply chain role that manages disposable supplies for a hospital environment. In this position, I have created and maintained spreadsheets for inventory tracking, ensuring accuracy and efficiency in supply management. I have also contributed to building training structures and have served as a team lead over a small group, helping guide others while maintaining organized records and documentation. Prior to my current role, I spent nearly five years working at Walmart, where I held multiple positions including cashier, Frozen/Dairy associate, unloading crew member, and overnight stocker.

Responsibilities

Each role required adaptability, strong work ethic, and the ability to collaborate with different teams. Working across various departments taught me how to associate easily with others, contribute as a team player, and remain dependable in fast paced environments. Additionally, some roles required independent work across separate buildings, which strengthened my ability to stay focused, organized, and self motivated. Through these experiences, I developed strong document keeping habits and learned how structured systems support both individual and team success.
